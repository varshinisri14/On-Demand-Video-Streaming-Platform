# Problem Statement

## 1. Title
FounderHub – On-Demand Video Streaming Platform

## 2. Domain
EdTech / Startup Ecosystem / Video Streaming

## 3. Who is the user?

### Entrepreneur
- Upload startup journey videos
- Upload pitch videos
- Manage startup profile

### Subscriber
- Purchase subscription
- Watch premium videos
- Bookmark videos

### Investor
- Browse startup profiles
- Watch pitch videos
- Express interest in investing

### Admin
- Approve users and videos
- Manage subscriptions
- Monitor active sessions

## 4. What problem are we solving?

Many aspiring entrepreneurs struggle to access reliable startup guidance and real-world entrepreneurial experiences in one place. Existing platforms either provide only educational content or only networking opportunities. Additionally, premium content is often affected by unauthorized account sharing, reducing platform revenue. FounderHub solves these problems by providing a secure subscription-based video streaming platform with intelligent session management.

## 5. Proposed Solution

FounderHub enables entrepreneurs to upload premium startup-related videos and pitch presentations. Subscribers can access exclusive content through a subscription, while investors can discover startups and express investment interest. The platform includes intelligent session management to prevent multiple simultaneous logins and protect premium content.

## 6. Core Entities / Database Tables

- Users
- EntrepreneurProfiles
- Videos
- Subscriptions
- Sessions
- BusinessPlans
- Bookmarks
- InvestorInterests

## 7. User Roles & Permissions

### Admin
- Manage users
- Approve videos
- Monitor sessions
- Manage subscriptions

### Entrepreneur
- Upload videos
- Manage profile
- Upload business plans

### Subscriber
- Subscribe
- Watch videos
- Bookmark videos

### Investor
- View startup profiles
- Watch pitch videos
- Send investment requests

## 8. Success Criteria

- User registration and login work successfully.
- Entrepreneurs can upload videos.
- Subscribers can watch premium videos after subscribing.
- Investors can send interest requests.
- Multiple simultaneous logins are detected and restricted.

## 9. Out of Scope

- Live video streaming
- Real payment gateway integration
- AI-based recommendations
- Video conferencing
- Online investment transactions
